PHOTOSYNTHESIS

Test for Starch

Demonstrate that starch is produced in leaves during photosynthesis.

01 Destarch the plant.
02 Expose the leaf to sunlight.
03 Test the leaf using iodine solution.
Observation The exposed portion turns blue-black.
02
☀️
PHOTOSYNTHESIS

Importance of Light

Show that sunlight is necessary for photosynthesis.

01 Cover part of a leaf.
02 Keep the plant in sunlight.
03 Perform the starch test.
Observation Only the exposed portion turns blue-black.

RESPIRATION

Fermentation by Yeast

Demonstrate the release of carbon dioxide during fermentation of sugar by yeast.

01 Mix sugar solution with yeast.
02 Connect the test tube to lime water.
03 Observe the lime water.
Observation Lime water turns milky.

NUTRITION

Nutrition in Amoeba

Understand how Amoeba obtains and digests its food.

01 Amoeba moves toward food.
02 Pseudopodia surround the food particle.
03 A food vacuole is formed.
Key idea Digestion takes place inside the food vacuole.

Remember These Key Ideas.

03
